# What is this project?
A personal project site where you can input foods that you ate that day, and it will return the sugar input. User will be able to input daily and/or weekly limits with warning reminders when they are coming close to those limits. 

# Why is this project?
For basically as long as I can remember, I've had a MASSIVE sweet tooth. As a child I was always asking my parents to buy me candy whenever we were at the check-out line of a store, or at the movie theatre getting popcorn, or even when I had to go to the hospital! Now that I'm an adult with my own money, however, I can go out and buy candy or pastries essentially whenever I want to. However, I'm very aware that this has led to me taking in WAY more sugar these past few years than I really should be. Especially since I have a family member who suffers from diabetes already and know how he's had to change his entire lifestyle and eating habits to deal with it.

This site is meant to keep me and others like me more concious of our sugar intake. It will me a more visual and tangible reminder to keep the amount of sugar you eat in check, as opposed to just trusting your own self control.
